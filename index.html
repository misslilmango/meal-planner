How the Planner Works

1. Build Your Recipe Box

Click the Recipes button in the top navigation to add your favourite meals. Organize and categorize them using options like Sheet Pan, Slow Cooker, Soup/Stew, Salad, and input ingredient measurements.

2. Schedule Your Week

Assign recipes to individual days of the week, or use the Autofill button to instantly randomize a weekly rotation. Past days are shaded and today is highlighted automatically.

3. Smart Grocery List & Scope Filters

Ingredients from your weekly schedule automatically aggregate into a shopping list. Use the scope tabs (Full Week, Rest of Week, Today Only) to control what items appear.

4. Pantry Stash & Cook Mode

Tap the archive icon next to any grocery item you already own to stash it away. Click Cook Mode on any meal to view full-screen instructions with a built-in screen awake lock.

5. Theme Colour Customiser

Click the Colour palette icon in the top header to open your browser's colour picker. Instantly customize the site's primary accent shade across all icons, buttons, and highlights.

6. How Memory & Backups Work

Local Browser Storage: All of your recipes, weekly meal plans, checked grocery items, and pantry stashes are saved inside your web browser's local memory. Your data stays private to your device.

Backup (Export) Button: Downloads a small .json file containing all your data to your computer or phone. Use this to save a copy of your custom recipes and schedule.

Restore (Import) Button: Uploads a previously saved backup .json file to load your recipes and settings back into the planner. Great for transferring your data across different devices or browsers.
